### Hi there! 👋

I'm Gianluigi, a **quantitative researcher** and **data scientist** working across machine learning, risk modelling and economic policy. I'm interested in building models that are not only accurate, but also interpretable and trustworthy enough to inform real decisions.

I work as a **senior consultant** at [Prometeia](https://www.prometeia.com/), where I develop and validate credit risk models for European banks. I also collaborate with the [European Central Bank](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/research/authors/profiles/gianluigi-lopardo.en.html), where I previously worked as a research analyst in the International Policy Analysis Division. Our recent work uses large language models to [forecast oil prices](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/conferences/shared/pdf/20260323_forecasting/Lopardo_poster.pdf) and to [study how firms' exposure to generative AI relates to their stock returns](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/pdf/scpwps/ecb.wp3093~458d28b4bc.en.pdf).

I hold a **PhD** from [Inria](https://inria.fr) and [Université Côte d'Azur](https://univ-cotedazur.fr/), where I studied the foundations of machine learning interpretability under the supervision of [Damien Garreau](https://sites.google.com/view/damien-garreau/home) and [Frédéric Precioso](https://webusers.i3s.unice.fr/~precioso/). My [thesis](https://theses.hal.science/tel-04917007/) received the [**2025 Young Researcher Prize**](https://math.univ-cotedazur.fr/laboratoire/news/phd-awards-0) from Métropole Nice Côte d'Azur. Before that, I earned an **MSc in mathematical engineering** and a **BSc in applied mathematics** from [Politecnico di Torino](https://www.polito.it/).

Away from work, I'm usually on my bike, hiking or [running](https://www.strava.com/athletes/95147093). Drop me a line if you're ever in Rome or [Brienza](https://www.discoverbrienza.it)!
