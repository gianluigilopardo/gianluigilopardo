### Hi there! 👋 
I'm Gianluigi, a **quantitative researcher** at the intersection of AI, risk and economics. 
I currently work as a **senior credit risk modeller** at [Prometeia](https://www.prometeia.com/) in Rome, where I develop and validate **IFRS 9 and regulatory credit models** for European banks. 
I also maintain an active research collaboration with the [European Central Bank](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/research/authors/profiles/gianluigi-lopardo.en.html) on **AI methods for economic research**. 
Before joining Prometeia, I was a research analyst at the [ECB](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/pub/research/authors/profiles/gianluigi-lopardo.en.html) in Frankfurt. 
I hold a **PhD** from [Inria](https://inria.fr/) and [Université Côte d'Azur](https://univ-cotedazur.fr/): my [thesis](https://theses.hal.science/tel-04917007) focused on the **foundations of machine learning interpretability**, under the supervision of [Damien Garreau](https://sites.google.com/view/damien-garreau/home) and [Frédéric Precioso](https://webusers.i3s.unice.fr/~precioso/). 
Previously, I earned an MSc in **mathematical engineering** and a BSc in **applied mathematics**, both from [Politecnico di Torino](https://www.polito.it/).
